A little about your Life Path

How these are calculated

Pythagorean system (A=1 … I=9, J=1 … R=9, S=1 … Z=8). Life Path: month, day and year are each reduced separately, then added and reduced. Expression: all letters; Soul Urge: vowels; Personality: consonants. Each name word is reduced on its own, then the results are added and reduced. Y counts as a vowel only when no vowel sits next to it. Master numbers 11, 22 and 33 are kept and shown with their base digit (e.g. 11/2).
